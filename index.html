Cancellation Radar

Hotel booking cancellation prediction · live demo

Hotels lose 37% of bookings to cancellations. This service scores each booking the moment it's made, explains the score with SHAP, and flags only the bookings worth a reminder call.

The hard part was honesty. Six columns are only filled in at check-in, so I dropped them (and proved two of them leak from the data). I tested on the newest 20% of bookings, not a random split. ROC-AUC fell from 0.954 to about 0.82 — the lower number is the real one.

Accident detection & emergency response

Final year project

A custom YOLOv12 model finds fire, moderate and severe accidents in uploaded images and video. A FastAPI backend stores incidents, a React dashboard shows them, and alerts go out by email or WhatsApp.

Tested on 1,800 held-out images: 89.9% mAP@50. Fire is the weak class — smoke has no clean edges — so that's where more data goes next.

Chest CT classification

Transfer learning with DVC and MLflow

VGG16 transfer learning to tell adenocarcinoma from normal CT scans, run as a four-stage DVC pipeline. My first version reported 100% accuracy — too good to be true.

The cause: training held out the first 20% of images, evaluation used the first 30%. A third of the "unseen" images had been trained on. One shared split constant fixed it.

  • VGG16
  • DVC
